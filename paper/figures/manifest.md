All figures and the audit/chain tables are generated by `scripts/make_paper_figures.py` (or `make tables`).
Audit figures and tables read their values directly from the verified run bundles listed below; the toy
figure is evaluated analytically from the parameters stated in its caption. `figure_sources.json` records
the source bundles, selected numerical summaries (audit totals, localization totals and unlocalized
remainders, chain summaries) and the toy parameters; individual heatmap cells are read from the bundles.
